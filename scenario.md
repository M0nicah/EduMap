### Use Case Scenario
A government education officer wants to identify the most critical public schools in Nairobi Province that need immediate intervention due to severe teacher shortages and poor student-teacher ratios.

#### 🔍 Filter Settings to Test
- Basic Filters:

-  Provinces: Select "Nairobi" only

School Status: Select "PUBLIC" only

Minimum Students: Set to 500 (to focus on larger schools)

#### Smart Filters:

Priority Level: Select "Critical"

Teacher Shortage Range: Set to 15-100 (schools needing at least 15 teachers)

Max Student-Teacher Ratio: Set to 50 (schools with ratios worse than 50:1)

District Comparison:

Compare two districts within Nairobi (e.g., Westlands vs Embakasi)
